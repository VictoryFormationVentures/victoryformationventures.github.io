A guided, hard-facts underwriting workflow for a land deal — Cover · Land · Site + Survey · Comparables · Land Cost · Team + Contacts · Neighborhood. Fill each section, then export an offer-memorandum-style presentation PDF.

Start / Resume

Land Acquisition Memo

Seven sections. No projections — just facts you can verify. The Land section auto-populates from Property Lookup (parcel, zoning, FEMA, setbacks). Comps come from a built-in CMA. Everything you enter is saved locally and to Google Sheets so any Land team member can pick it up.

Launch Workflow →
What's inside
  1. Cover — property, address, memo date
  2. Land — lot size, zoning, setbacks, FEMA, utilities, HOA
  3. Site + Survey — photos + attached survey PDF
  4. Comparables — 3–4 sold comps with $/sqft breakdown
  5. Land Cost — contract price, EM, closing costs, DD + close dates
  6. Team + Contacts — buyer's agent, seller, listing agent, attorney
  7. Neighborhood — map + optional context
