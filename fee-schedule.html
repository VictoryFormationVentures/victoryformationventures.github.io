Billing rules

  • Flat-fee work: 50% at engagement, 50% at delivery. Retainers billed on the 1st.
  • Hourly work invoiced twice monthly, net 10. Two-hour minimum per engagement.
  • City fees, engineering, survey, legal, and printing are passed through at cost with no markup; we pre-bill them.
  • Rush delivery (under 5 business days on reports): +25%. Travel beyond 40 miles of Austin or Nashville billed at the IRS rate plus time.
  • Report fees are credited toward brokerage or development fees when we transact on the same property within 12 months.
  • Deal partners on our lot programs receive the Property Diagnostic and Land Comp Report at no charge.

Required disclosures

  • Comp reports and opinions of value are broker price opinions, not appraisals, and are labeled as such. Texas prohibits license holders from calling a BPO or CMA an "appraised value."
  • Brokerage services are provided through a sponsoring Texas broker; commissions are negotiable and set in the listing or buyer representation agreement.
  • Consulting fees are separate from and in addition to any commission, and are disclosed to all parties when we also act as buyer or agent.
  • Rezoning and variance fees cover our management of the case; approval is never guaranteed and success fees are paid only on approval.
  • Nashville engagements are contracted through The Stonehouse Company under the same schedule.
